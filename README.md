# CrimeGPT — AI-Powered Legal & Investigative Intelligence Workspace

CrimeGPT is an end-to-end criminal intelligence platform designed for law enforcement officers, prosecutors, and legal researchers. Powered by Google Gemini AI, CrimeGPT automates the extraction, structuring, and legal analysis of complex crime scene documentation.

## Key Features

- 📝 **AI Incident Narrative Generation**: Transforms raw officer audio transcripts, wiretap notes, and field observations into structured, chronological law enforcement reports with evidence logs and suspect descriptions.
- ⚖️ **Prosecutorial & Penal Code Audit**: Analyzes crime reports against statutory elements of offenses, evaluates evidentiary strength (0–100%), and highlights evidentiary gaps or constitutional risks (Fourth/Fifth Amendment concerns).
- 📜 **Automated Search Warrant Affidavits**: Generates court-ready legal affidavits and probable cause declarations for judicial filing.
- 💬 **Legal Advisor Chat**: Real-time legal intelligence assistant referencing penal codes, procedural precedents (*Terry v. Ohio*, *Miranda v. Arizona*), and investigative directives.
- 📂 **Case Record Vault**: Local and Firebase-backed persistence for managing active crime reports and investigative files.
